# Safety

Surface these hazards **before** any instructions:

- **Flex-cable surgery** — fine-pitch connectors tear easily; use a hot bar or proper FPC tool, not a soldering iron on the cable itself.
- **LiPo packs** — charge in a fire-safe bag, never unattended, observe polarity, stop at 3.0 V/cell discharge.
- **405 nm UV** — eye/skin hazard; use UV-blocking glasses, never look into the emitter.
- **Contact lenses** — dichoptic color induces chromostereopsis in human observers; it does not improve machine sensing. Do not present as a sensor enhancement.
